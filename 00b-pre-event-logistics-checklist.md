# Pre-Event Logistics Checklist

Housekeeping to sort out **before 25 Nov**, separate from the technical prep. The guideline is
explicit that the organizers will issue the environment, access, and task details on the day — this
checklist is about making sure your team isn't caught flat-footed by logistics, not about guessing the
technical tasks.

## Confirm with organizers (via the ticketing system, once available, or directly)

- [ ] What exactly is preloaded on the MCMC-provided laptops — is any tooling already installed, or
      are participants expected to bring their own on USB/download it during setup?
- [ ] What access path will be used to reach assigned systems (VPN, jump box, direct network access) —
      Section 5 of the guideline says this will be assigned, but confirm the mechanics if unclear.
- [ ] Internet access availability on the provided laptops — this affects whether tools like VirusTotal
      lookups, downloading additional tooling mid-event, or searching public CVE databases will even be
      possible.
- [ ] Exact submission format and deadline for Day 1 remediation work and Day 2 forensic conclusions
      (Section 6.4.1 says not to assume a format that hasn't been given).
- [ ] How the ticketing system works and how to access it — the briefing on Day 1 (09:00–09:30 per the
      agenda) should cover this, but confirm you know how to reach it before then.

## Team logistics

- [ ] Confirm each team member's laptop/equipment situation for Day 1 morning (registration is
      08:30–09:00, briefing 09:00–09:30, competition starts 09:45 — tight window if anything goes
      wrong with laptop setup).
- [ ] Agree on the role split in `templates/team-roles-workflow.md` before the event, not during.
- [ ] Confirm everyone has reviewed `00-rules-and-reminders.md`, especially the AI policy (Section 7)
      and scope rules (Section 6.1) — a rules violation risks the team's standing more than a missed
      technical finding.
- [ ] Decide how your team will take notes during the event (shared doc, physical notebook, etc.) given
      that AI tools are off-limits for anything exercise-related once it starts.

## Physical / personal prep

- [ ] Power banks / chargers for the two-day event.
- [ ] Note the agenda's flexible break times (10:15–10:30, 12:30–14:00, 15:30–15:45 on Day 1) so the
      team can plan rotation/rest without losing track of the clock.
- [ ] Day 2 starts with registration/readiness verification at 08:30 — don't assume Day 1's setup
      carries over, since the guideline states Day 2 "stands on its own."

## What NOT to do beforehand

- Don't assume specific tasks, targets, or a timetable beyond what's in the official agenda — the
  guideline explicitly asks participants not to do this.
- Don't prepare pre-written "solutions" to guessed challenges — focus prep time on methodology and
  tool fluency (the rest of this repo) instead.
